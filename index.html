Forge the Day Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

This policy applies to Forge the Day version 0.1.0, the private M4I provider-free beta. Forge the Day is a local-first productivity beta for adults.

Information the app handles

The app lets you enter a display name, roles, goals, success conditions, non-negotiables, time zone, check-in windows, coaching-firmness preference, proposals, commitments, reasons, and related local history. It also lets you make a bounded voice recording for local playback.

These records can contain personal or sensitive information depending on what you choose to enter or say. Use synthetic, non-private information during M4I testing and record only with everyone’s consent.

Where information is stored

M4I stores application records in the app’s local database on the iPhone. Voice recordings stay in app-private temporary storage for review and playback. A recording is removed when you cancel it, explicitly delete it, leave the capture screen, or next start the app after an orphaned capture.

Forge the Day does not add application-layer encryption to its local database. The iPhone operating-system sandbox, device lock, and normal storage protection provide the applicable device protections. This beta does not claim production-grade confidentiality or crash-proof secure erasure. Apple-controlled device backups or diagnostics may be governed by your Apple settings and Apple’s policies rather than by Forge the Day.

Information collected or transmitted by Forge the Day

The M4I beta does not collect or transmit your app content to Forge the Day or a third party. It has no Forge account, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, tracking, pairing, upload, remote transcription, or AI/provider request. Each installation keeps separate application state and does not synchronize with another installation or a Forge cloud store.

M4I does not share app content with third parties. If third-party access is introduced later, Forge the Day will update this policy before that access begins and require protections at least equivalent to those described here.

Apple distributes the beta through TestFlight. TestFlight may make tester feedback, session, and crash information available to the developer under Apple’s terms. Apple’s handling of App Store, TestFlight, device, diagnostic, and feedback information is governed by Apple’s own terms, settings, and privacy policies. This is separate from transmitting Forge app content.

Microphone access

Forge the Day asks for microphone access only when you choose to start a voice recording. A recording is limited to five minutes and 8 MiB, is foreground-only, and remains temporary. Moving the app to the background or locking the screen stops recording and may leave a partial recording available for local review or deletion. The M4I build cannot upload or transcribe the recording. You can revoke microphone permission through the applicable iOS Settings at any time.

Your choices and deletion

You can cancel or delete a voice recording from the capture flow. You can use Reset local prototype data to delete the confirmed profile, goals, commitments, event history, receipts, and onboarding draft from the app’s local database. Uninstalling the app removes its local app container, subject to Apple-controlled backup and restore behavior.

M4I has no developer-operated account or cloud copy from which Forge the Day can retrieve, export, correct, or delete your local records. Manage those records on the device and manage Apple backups through the applicable Apple settings.

Children and professional advice

Forge the Day M4I is intended for adults and requires an acknowledgement that the tester is 18 or older. It is a productivity prototype, not therapy, medical care, crisis support, legal advice, or financial advice. Do not use it to record children or other people without appropriate notice and consent.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated before Forge the Day changes the data it handles, adds remote processing or third parties, or expands beyond the private M4I beta. The effective date above identifies the published version.
